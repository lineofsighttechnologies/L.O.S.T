Who We Are

Line Of Sight Technologies is a Zimbabwe-based ICT and security solutions provider dedicated to empowering homes, businesses, and institutions across the World. We offer a full spectrum of services, including IT support, system and network administration, CCTV, access control, web front-end development, and hardware & software procurement. We combine innovation with reliability to deliver complete peace of mind.

Our team of certified technicians and experts brings deep industry expertise and a customer-first approach to every project. Whether you're upgrading your surveillance system, building a secure network, or seeking professional IT consulting, we tailor our comprehensive solutions to meet your unique needs
